Description

I'm a Brazilian developer living in Copenhagen, Denmark, and I make the games I'd like to play. The first of them is The Last Good Boy, which is about my Shih Tzu, Ramon. I hope you enjoy it.

History

Why this name?

I always used the nick "vkaike" in games, since my name is Victor Kaike. But I lost the password to that account once and created another one called "vkaike2", and I ended up liking the way it sounded, mostly because the abbreviation "vk2" in English comes out sounding like "Pikachu".

Why make games?

I spent a big part of my childhood and teenage years playing on the Super Nintendo and the early MMOs, and I understand how much emotion a game can stir in a person. I remember the frustration of not getting past a certain boss, and the joy I felt when I finally got an item, after spending a whole week farming.

After playing so many games, I started getting more interested in understanding why the developer made decision X instead of decision Y. Sometimes I would spend the whole night thinking about it, almost as if it were a conversation with the game designer. After years of thinking about that, I started really wanting to create my own game. And since I have always liked drawing and playing the guitar (even though I have not played in a long time) and I chose software development as my profession, the most obvious option was to start developing my games.
