Description

The best Idle RPG inspired by a dog named Ramon of the World! But seriously, you play as a brown shih-tzu named Ramon, and your goal is to find your friends, but that won't be an easy task. First you need to get ready by gathering resources, unlocking the map, fighting monsters and taking their loot, and finally finding them all, proving that you are the best dog in the world!

Why did I make this game?

It all started when I looked at Ramon's life (my dog) and realized it was a lot like an RPG. Let me give you some examples:

He has several toys and he plays differently depending on the toy, which could easily be turned into weapons. He also has several little outfits that we bought for the cold, which could be the armor. On top of that, he has a daily quest: taking him out for a walk every day!

Along with that, I always wanted him to be immortalized, since dogs unfortunately live shorter lives than we do :( So the most obvious choice was to make a game inspired by him. And since I was watching "The Last of Us" at the time, I thought: what if I made "The Last Good Boy"? And then the next step was creating the story behind that name.

Content

  • Between 30 and 40 hours of gameplay
  • 133 items to collect
  • 12 monsters to defeat
  • 3 bosses to face
  • 13 different resources to gather
  • Equipment shows up on your character!
  • Item enchantment system, to optimize your character
  • Fully localized in 9 languages, including Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Simplified Chinese
