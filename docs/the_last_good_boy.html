Description

The best Idle RPG inspired by a dog named Ramon! Now, seriously, you play as a brown shih tzu called Ramon, and your goal is to find his friends, but that task will not be easy. First you need to get ready by gathering resources, unlocking the map, fighting monsters and being the best dog in the world!

History

It all started when I began looking at Ramon's life (my dog) and realised it was a lot like an RPG. Let me give you some examples. He has several toys and he plays differently depending on the toy, which could easily be adapted into weapons. He also has several little outfits (and he looks very cute wearing them, by the way), and those could be the armour. On top of that, he has a daily quest, where I have to take him out for a walk every day!

Along with that, I always wanted him to be immortalised, since dogs unfortunately live shorter lives than we do :(. So the most obvious choice was to make a game inspired by him. And since I was watching "The Last of Us" at the time, I thought: what if I made "The Last Good Boy"? And then the next step was creating the story behind that name.

Content

  • Between 30 and 40 hours of gameplay
  • 133 items to collect
  • 12 monsters to defeat
  • 3 bosses to face
  • 13 different resources to gather
  • Equipment shows up on your character!
  • Item enchantment system, to optimise your character
  • Fully localised in 9 languages, including Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Simplified Chinese
