Descrição

vkaike2 is a one-person game studio run by a Brazilian developer based in Copenhagen, Denmark, making small, cozy games about the things worth being sentimental over. Its first game, The Last Good Boy, is about a dog named Ramon.

História

How it started

vkaike2 started in October 2024 as a way to make one specific game. I am a Brazilian developer living in Copenhagen, and I had a small Shih Tzu named Ramon who was, as far as I am concerned, the goodest boy in the world. Building a game around him — his toys as weapons, his outfits as armour — turned out to be the project I actually wanted to finish, so I made a studio to finish it under.

What comes next

The Last Good Boy releases on 4 September 2026. The studio remains a one-person operation, working on cozy games that are small enough to be finished and personal enough to be worth making.
